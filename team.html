Founder's Story

Meet Muhammad Saqib

Founder & CEO · Intermediate · Full-Stack Developer

Growing up in Karachi, Saqib watched his brightest friends struggle to find jobs despite perfect academic records. The problem wasn't talent — it was information asymmetry. Students from tier-1 colleges had access to career counselors, industry networks, and mentorship. Everyone else had to guess.

"I didn't build SkillGap Pro to make money. I built it because I'm tired of watching talented people fail simply because nobody showed them the map."

Our Values

What Drives Us Every Day

These 6 values aren't just words on a wall. They guide every product decision, every hire, and every line of code we write.

Student-First Always

Every feature we build starts with a real student problem. No vanity metrics, no feature bloat.

Radical Honesty

We tell you the truth about your skill gaps — even when it's uncomfortable. That's how real growth happens.

Access for All

Career clarity shouldn't be a privilege. SkillGap Pro is free, forever, for every learner in India.

Ship Fast, Learn Faster

We release weekly. We iterate based on user feedback. Perfection is the enemy of student progress.

Quality Obsession

Every role template is verified against real job postings. Every recommendation is written by certified coaches.

Privacy by Design

Your data stays yours. We use local storage, encrypt everything, and never sell user information.
