Our Story

Born from a Real Problem

In 2023, our founders watched thousands of talented students graduate with strong academics but zero clarity about what employers actually wanted. They had degrees, but no roadmap. They had motivation, but no mirror to show them their real skill gaps.

Career counselors were expensive. Online assessments were generic. Job descriptions were overwhelming. So we asked: what if we built a single platform that compares your current skills to a real role, shows you exactly what's missing, and gives you a personalized plan to close the gap — all for free?

That question became SkillGap Pro. Today it's used by 25,000+ learners across India, and we're just getting started.
