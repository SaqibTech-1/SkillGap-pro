The Engine

How Our Matching System Works

Our algorithm uses three layers of comparison to give you the most honest, accurate match score possible. It's not keyword stuffing — it's semantic matching.

Exact Skill Matching

First, we check if your entered skills match any of the 10 required skills for your target role. These become your "Strong Skills" — weighted at 100%.

Partial & Synonym Matching

Next, we detect partial overlaps — like "JS" matching "JavaScript", or "Py" matching "Python". These become "Improvement Areas" — weighted at 50%.

Gap Identification

Everything not matched in the first two layers becomes a "Missing Skill" — the exact gaps you need to close to become job-ready.

Score Calculation

Your match percentage is calculated as:

Score = ((Strong + 0.5 × Improvement) / Total) × 100

The Dashboard

Every Widget, Explained

Your analysis dashboard is packed with actionable insights. Here's what each section means and how to use it.

Match Score Donut

The circular progress chart shows your overall job-readiness percentage at a glance. Green zone (75%+) means you can start applying. Orange means 1-2 skills away. Red means more learning needed.

Strong Skills Card

Skills you already have that match the role's requirements. These are your strengths — highlight them in your resume and interview answers. Leverage them to negotiate better.

Missing Skills Card

Critical skills required for the role that you don't have yet. These are your highest-priority learning targets. Focus on the top 3 first for maximum impact.

Improvement Areas

Skills where you have partial knowledge — like knowing basic JavaScript but not React. These are quick wins. A focused weekend course can push them into "Strong".

Recommendations

Curated, role-specific action items like "Build 3 React projects" or "Earn Google Ads certification". Each recommendation is tied to the gaps detected in your analysis.

PDF Export

One-click downloadable report with all your analysis data, beautifully formatted. Share it with mentors, attach to job applications, or keep it as a personal milestone record.
